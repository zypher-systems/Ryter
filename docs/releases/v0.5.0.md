A second opinion before you commit: `/second` asks a different model to review your changes. You choose the model and how much one review may spend, and it never spends past that.

## Upgrade

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/ryter/main/install.sh | sh
```

## `/second`

Type `/second` in any solo hat. A model other than the one doing the work reads your uncommitted changes and what you asked for, and reports what it finds, each finding marked **blocking** or **note**, with a verdict and what the review cost. The model you work with gets the findings with your next message, so "fix those" works.

It works under the review hat's rules: it can read files and run your tests, and it can't change, install, or format anything. It creates no files.

## You choose, once

The first `/second` opens a chooser:

- **Every model you have a key for**, from each provider's live catalog, so new models appear the day they're listed.
- **What this review should cost with each one, in dollars**, from the model's price and the size of your change.
- **Facts, not recommendations.**
  - The model doing the work isn't offered: it can't be its own second opinion.
  - Models that can't use tools aren't offered.
  - A model from the same vendor as yours is marked as less independent.
  - A model with no known price can't be chosen, because no limit could hold it.

Nothing is preselected. After the model, you type the most one review may spend, in dollars; there's no preset for that either. A large change reviewed by a strong model can cost $20 or more, so set what you're willing to pay. The choice is saved, and `/second model` changes it.

## Every review asks first

```
x-ai/grok-4.7 on openrouter (your choice)
reviews 2 files, +8 −1, read-only
about $0.02–$0.31 of your $5.00 limit
your last 4 reviews with it cost $0.03–$0.19
```

`n` spends nothing. The last line appears once you have a history with that model, and it's the estimate to trust most.

## Your limit is kept before spending, not after

- **Each step is priced before it's sent,** from what it will send. A step that would pass your limit isn't sent.
- **When about one step's room is left, or three quarters of the limit is spent,** the reviewer is told to stop exploring and write up what it has. You get findings for what you paid, marked if the review was cut short.
- **If your saved reviewer is retired or loses its price,** the chooser comes back. Ryter never switches to another model on its own.

## Also

- **The solo prompt, the builder, and the auditor say that `node -e` is refused** along with `python -c`, as the gate has done since 0.4.0.

## Tried before release

In the TUI, on `deepseek-v4.1-flash` over OpenRouter, with no reviewer chosen:

1. **The chooser.** `/second` opened it with 389 models from the live catalog (tool-less models left out), each priced for the change. Free models read `$0`. Typing `grok-4.7` narrowed it to one row, `$0.01–$0.23`. The limit was `1`, and it was saved to `~/.ryter/review.toml` as typed.
2. **The prompt.** `reviews 1 file, +5 −0, read-only · about $0.01–$0.22 of your $1.00 limit`.
3. **The review.** A new `median` sorted numbers as strings and returned the upper middle for even-length lists. grok-4.7 flagged both as blocking, plus the missing test, for **$0.02**. The project was unchanged.
4. **The fix.** In an earlier run, "fix the blocking problems from the second opinion and add tests" had the working model fix both, and `npm test` passed 8 of 8.

The first try of step 1 found a bug before release: the review had created `ROADMAP.md` and `DECISIONS.md` in the project, then counted them as changes to review. It creates no files now, and a test holds it to that.
