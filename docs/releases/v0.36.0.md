The *All hats* seat in `/models` lists every connection you have a key for, as each hat's seat does, and a model chosen there from another connection moves the session to it.

## Upgrade

```sh
ryter update
```

Coming from 0.35.0:

- **All hats lists every connection.** With two keyed connections, each hat's seat listed both connections' models and *All hats* listed the session's connection alone. Now every seat has the same list, every keyed connection's models, each row naming its connection. `/models` asks every connection for its list as it opens.
- **A model from another connection moves the session there.** Choosing a row on *All hats* sets that model for every hat on the row's connection: the key, the prices, the saved route, the model check and the live connection all move with it, and the chat line says so (`model · claude-opus-5.5 · using anthropic`), followed as before by which hats had their own model and now follow. `/model <id>` typed by hand still means the current connection.
- **One id on two connections stays the session's own.** A model listed by two connections (Claude through Anthropic and through OpenRouter) has two prices and two windows; opening `/models` applies the session's connection's row to the session, never the other's, and the price book prices the session's turns from its own connection's list.
- **A connection's own list refreshes its rows alone.** The list startup asks for, or `/provider` choosing a connection, replaces that connection's rows in `/models` and keeps the others', so the seats never shrink to one connection; the rows of a connection whose key was removed go with the next list.
- **The price book takes the fresh list.** A session's turns were priced from the connection's last list, kept from an earlier session, even after the fresh one came; now the book is built again from the fresh list as it lands.
- **"Loading" means it.** The panel says "loading" until every keyed connection's list has come or has failed, not until the first one lands, so the other connections are never shown as complete on their built-in rows. A connection whose list comes back empty loses its rows and its cached list. With no keyed connection at all, the panel keeps its built-in rows and stops saying "loading". A connection that didn't answer keeps its note until it answers again, not until some other connection's list lands.
- **A list without the session's model prices it from the book.** When the session's connection answers without the model in use (an account's filter dropped it, or the list came back empty), the catalog price goes with the row: the header and the saved route show what the turns are charged at, which may be nothing.
- **A chosen row's context length is the gauge's.** Choosing a model in `/models` sets the context gauge from the row's own length. Before, an id Ryter did not know by name (OpenRouter's `x-ai/grok-4.7`) was put at 200k over the length the row had just shown.
- **A connection added this session is listed.** A connection added in `/provider` is one `/models` lists and the session can use at once. Before, the picker waited on its list for good, and using it said "unknown connection" until the next start.
- **The `default` row names the connection the session is on now,** after *All hats* moved it, not the one it was on when the panel opened.
- **The saved route is the header.** `~/.ryter/last.toml` now records the price the header shows, an unpriced model included, so a price the list dropped does not come back at the next start.
- **The header is priced as the turns are charged.** Choosing a model, or a list landing, prices the header from the same book the session's turns are charged from: the connection's own list first, the shipped and pulled tables behind it. Before, Enter could show the table's price while the turns ran at the list's.
- **A connection removed mid-fetch is gone.** Removing a connection in `/provider` while its list was still coming no longer puts its rows back, writes its cache or leaves a note when the answer lands.
- **One fetch per connection.** Opening `/models` while startup's list for the same connection is still out no longer fetches it twice.

## Why

The user, on 0.35.0 with Anthropic in use and OpenRouter keyed: "each of the hats shows my anthropic key models plus the open router models which is perfect. But if I go to all hats it only shows the anthropic models?" A hat has carried a connection with its model since the per-hat seats; *All hats* kept the one-connection shape from before 0.31.0, so the only way to another connection's models for every hat was `/provider` first. "It needs to work the same as each hat."

## Good to know

- The seats column shows each seat's model, not its connection; the rows in the list do.
- Open from earlier releases: the `/sessions` panel's columns and its `$0.00` for an unpriced session, the README's screenshots of the previous screen, the light theme's slab untried by a person, a card not dissolving on closing.

## Tried before release

The full local check and CI. The real binary driven offline with two keyed connections on the simulated provider: both rows under *All hats*, the second connection's row chosen, the line `model · fixture-model · using second`, and the model check and the next turn both sent with the second connection's key. Then with the two connections listing the same model id at different context windows: the sidebar kept the session's connection's window after `/models` opened, and *All hats* still listed both connections after `/provider` chose one. Then with a stale cached list at one price and the live list at another: the picker opened on the cached list ("from 3 h ago · refreshing"), and the turn after the refresh was charged at the live price. Then a cached price for a model the live list no longer carried: the turn before the list was charged at the cached price, the turn after was unpriced, and the saved route dropped the price with the row; a prefixed id chosen from the list put its own 131k on the gauge; and a connection added in `/provider` mid-session was used at once and listed by `/models` beside the other, with the list request carrying its key. Then the route after a dropped model carried no price, a model the list priced at $4 and the shipped table knows with its cache tiers was charged and shown at the table's $1 on both sides, `/models` during a held startup fetch asked once, and a connection removed while its list was out came back as nothing.
