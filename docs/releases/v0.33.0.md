The screen softens: one rail at the right with the hats at its top, dotted hairlines, cards as slabs, a dial for the context, round ends on the chip, and motion after the frame.

## Upgrade

```sh
ryter update
```

Coming from 0.32.0:

- **One rail.** The left rail and the three-pane layout are gone. From 100 columns one column sits at the right, 28 wide there and 44 from 132: the project and its branch, the hats (the one you wear with its model, what it may do and its turns; the others with their model and cost), the turns, the context, the spend per hat, the uncommitted files and the plan. The conversation reads from the left edge. `F2` hides the rail; `F6` and `F7` move between the conversation and the rail, where `↑↓` walk the hats and `Enter` puts one on in place.
- **Nothing is said twice.** The Activity block is gone: the hat rows name the model, and a running turn carries its own sign on its status row in the conversation, a square turning through its quadrants in the hat's colour beside a band of light crossing the words. The braille spinner is retired.
- **Fewer right angles.** The hairlines between the conversation and the rail are dotted. A card or a panel is a slab on its own tint with cut corners and no shadow, its title in the first row; in sixteen colours and without colour the rounded line box stays. The turn's spine is dotted and its closing join is rounded. A gauge is a thick line with a cap on a dashed track.
- **Round ends on the hat chip and the view tab.** They take the Powerline half-circles. `[ui] caps` decides it: `auto`, the default, draws them in kitty, WezTerm, Ghostty, iTerm2 and Windows Terminal, which have the glyphs whatever the font, and whenever `nerd_font` is on; `round` and `square` decide by hand, and `/settings` carries the key. A terminal without the glyph shows a box in the cap's place, and `square` is then the honest choice.
- **The context is a dial.** On a screen of 28 rows or more the rail draws the context window as a ring of braille dots with the percentage in its middle and the counts beside it; shorter screens keep the line gauge.
- **Motion.** A card fades in from the hat's colour, a hat switch sweeps the chip in the new colour, and a fresh session's welcome gathers itself. Colour effects run in truecolor; nothing moves under `NO_COLOR`.
- **Ratatui 0.30.** The screen library and crossterm are current; the motion comes from the tachyonfx crate.

## Why

The first developers to use Ryter said the way it works is good and the way it looks is a toy. This release is the look: one place beside the conversation to read, the text at the left edge the way every editor reads, nothing drawn twice, and as few right angles as a grid of cells allows. Each step was drawn on a canvas beside the screen as it was, and built after the user chose.

## Good to know

- With the rail hidden by `F2` the hats are off the screen too, since there is one column now; the chip at the prompt still names the hat you wear and `Tab` still switches it.
- The Hats page on `Alt+2` is unchanged and keeps every hat's full card.
- The README's screenshots still show the previous screen with two rails; new ones are owed.
- The light theme's slab is a white card on light grey and has not been looked at by a person yet.
- A card does not dissolve as it closes.

## Tried before release

The full local check and CI. The real binary driven offline at 132 columns: the sweep, the fade and the gathering change over the first frames and settle; the band crosses the status row; the ring, the spend rows and the square draw; `F7`, `↓` and `Enter` put a hat on with the chip following; `[ui] caps = "round"` puts the half-circles on the chip. The user ran the dev build on their own terminal through the softer screen and the round ends and asked for the spend rows and the one rail that are in this release.
