Audits come to you: after a build turn changes your files, Ryter offers one. `/second` is now `/audit`.

## Upgrade

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/ryter/main/install.sh | sh
```

## Offered after your changes

When a build turn finishes having changed files, Ryter asks:

```
Audit this work before you commit?
z-ai/glm-5.3 on openrouter (your choice)
reviews 1 file, +6 −0, read-only
about $0.01–$0.20 of your $2.00 limit

y · audit   n · not now   s · stop offering
```

- **`y`** runs the audit. The offer already showed the cost, so there's no second question.
- **`n`** passes, and your build turn's summary stays where it was.
- **`s`** stops the offers. Turn them back on under audit offers in `/settings`, or with `[ui] offer_audit = true`.

Nothing is offered after a turn that only talked or read, a turn that was cancelled or cut short, or when there's nothing uncommitted. If nobody has been chosen to audit yet, the offer asks whether to choose one first.

## Audits stay in the chat, marked as audits

An audit used to arrive looking like the rest of the chat. A long one filled the pane, its header scrolled away, and the work it audited went off screen. Now:
- **Every line of an audit carries a rule in the auditor's color,** so any part of it on screen reads as the audit, not the model at work.
- **A long audit folds to its first 14 lines.** Blocking findings come first, and the verdict is in its header. `^o` shows it whole, as it does for edits.

## `/audit`

`/second` is now `/audit`, which says what it does. `/second` still works, and `/audit model` changes who audits and your limit. Everything from 0.5.0 still holds: you choose the model and the limit, every audit asks before it spends, and it never goes past your limit.

## Tried before release

In the TUI, on `deepseek-v4.1-flash`, with `z-ai/glm-5.3` chosen to audit at $2.00:

1. **"Add a median function."** When the turn ended, the offer appeared with the cost and the history (`your last 2 reviews with it cost $0.08`). `y` ran the audit straight away: no blocking problems, and one note about empty input.
2. **"Add a mode function."** The offer appeared again. `s` stopped it and saved `offer_audit = false`.
3. **"Add a range function."** The function was added, and no offer followed.
4. **`/audit` and `/second`.** Both asked before running, and `n` said `audit not run`.
5. **The audit in the chat.** After "add median, mode and range", the audit appeared under the build turn's summary. Every line carried the auditor's rule, and it folded after 14 lines, with the builder's summary still on screen above it.
