The workspace is the only screen, and the polish before a review: the ledger and classic layouts and the `^t` workbench are gone, every panel sits on a cleared band, no cost is ever a fake `$0.00`, the hint bar carries the open card's own keys, and the light and sixteen-colour screens read.

## Upgrade

```sh
ryter update
```

Coming from 0.29.0:

- **One screen.** The ledger layout (0.6.0's reading column), the classic layout (the 0.5 screen) and the `^t` workbench are removed; `[ui] layout` is gone, and a settings file that still names a layout loads. `/changes` opens the changes panel everywhere. The 0.27.0 note said the old layouts stay available; this reverses it, on the author's call. Two things the workspace had lost on the way are back: a click on the status row opens the reasoning pane, and `NO_COLOR` leaves every cell reset (no drop shadow without colour, a reverse-video chip).
- **Panels sit on a cleared band.** The rows a panel occupies are cleared across the body before it is drawn, so the chat's gutter and line ends no longer touch its borders at 80 and 120 columns.
- **No fake zeros.** The spend panel's rows, its title and total, the session's label, the project's total and this-month rows, and the inspector's project block use the three-place form the hats use: a build that cost $0.004 says so, never `$0.00`. A new session says `$0`; `$?.??` is for a run that had no price.
- **The hint bar carries the open card's keys.** Under the plan panel it says approve, adjust and reject; under the audit card, repair; `a edits` only on an allow card.
- **Light theme and sixteen colours.** The light theme's section labels read (held at 4.5:1 by the theme test). In sixteen colours the hat that is on keeps the sidebar's background, marked by its bar and bold name, instead of a grey fill its colour could not be read on.
- **Smaller.** The prompt's chip keeps its colour under a panel. The commit panel says how many files are out of sight on a short screen. A Hats page too short for a hat's card names the hat on one line. The hats have their own heading in the slash menu. Every tool has a short verb (`file_audit` read `file_a…`). The round cap's settings label fits. `doctor` says what its unknown-price check means. The README's screenshots are of the workspace screen.

## Why

A content creator asked to review Ryter. Two polish passes at 80, 120 and 176 columns in three themes found the warts above, and once the workspace was the screen a reviewer would see, keeping two older screens alive behind a flag meant testing every rule twice for nobody.

## Good to know

- The old design contracts, `docs/sidebar-design.md` and `docs/hat-rack-design.md`, stay in the repository marked retired.
- Two edges on the workspace predate this release and are still there: at 80 columns the top bar's title runs into the status text, and the hint bar never shows `^c` or `esc`.
- The light and sixteen-colour screens have been measured in renders, not yet looked at by a person on a real terminal.
