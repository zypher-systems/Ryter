A long session no longer wears out its own context: each compaction now comes out the same size, however many came before it.

## Upgrade

```sh
ryter update
```

Coming from 0.44.1:

- **Compaction holds its size.** When the conversation fills 85% of the model's window, Ryter folds the older messages into an extract and keeps your last four messages whole. The extract used to carry the last one word for word, so it grew with every pass. In a long session it reached the point where a pass could no longer get back under the line: every round compacted again, the prompt cache was thrown away each time, and the request was finally refused. The extract is now held to a tenth of the window, and the last one is folded in, not copied.
- **Your words go last.** When the extract is over its size, the model's notes and Ryter's own (a check's result, "not done") are shortened and then dropped, oldest first, before any of your messages. A line in the extract says how many were left out.
- **Older writes stop going out whole.** A `write` carries its whole file, and every round after it sent that file to the model again. Older calls' arguments over 2 KB are now shortened like older tool output, keeping the file's path. The newest call stays whole.
- **Your four turns are yours.** The four turns compaction keeps now count only your messages. Ryter's messages to the model counted too, so fewer of your turns were kept than the setting says.
- **A session compacted before this** carries an extract in the old form. Its next compaction folds it the same way.

## Why

A review of the harness function by function found it: the loop, tools, context, permissions, memory, subagents, hooks and telemetry. Context was the weakest, and this was the fault in it. `DECISIONS.md` 2026-11-07.

## Good to know

- Compaction still writes no summary: the extract is what was said, cut to fit, and a pass costs nothing.
- `/compact` forces a pass as before. A forced pass with little to take can leave the size as it was.

## Tried before release

The full local check and CI on Linux and macOS. Five new tests, two of which failed on 0.44.1: a 600-turn session compacted over and over, and older writes shortened in both of the forms they're sent in. The real TUI with the simulated provider: seven 25 KB writes, `/compact` twice, one extract after each, older writes down from 27 KB to 1.7 KB, the newest whole, no provider errors.
