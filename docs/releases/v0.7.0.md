## What was wrong

- **The crew board could sit still for ten minutes.** A lane changed only when a worker finished a tool call. A model thinking for two minutes, or writing a 200-line edit, looked exactly like a model that had stalled.
- **In solo mode, Ryter's name wasn't on the screen.** The costs were in the bottom bar, and the hat was a small chip at the bar's left end, easy to miss.

## What changed

Both layouts were picked from eight options on a design canvas: four for solo, four for crew.

**Live lanes (crew)**
- **Each worker has a card that updates a few times a second.** Its first row says who is working, on which task, with which model, and what it is doing right now, for how long:
  - `WAITING`: the model hasn't answered yet. After 20 seconds the card says the provider is slow.
  - `THINKING`: the model is reasoning.
  - `WRITING`: the model is writing a file edit or its reply.
  - `RUNNING`: a tool or command is running.
- **Under the first row is a meter:** lines of the file written so far, tokens (marked `~`, estimated until the step ends), tokens per second, the task's cost, and its tool calls.
- **Under the meter are the last three lines of what the worker is producing:** its reasoning, the file taking shape, or a test run's output as it runs.
- **`^r` on the board hides the reasoning** and shows it again.
- **A new PULSE tile** shows the crew's tokens per second, a bar for each second, and how long since any worker last sent anything. It turns yellow after 30 seconds of silence.
- **With no budget set, SPEND shows what the last minute cost.**
- **On a short screen,** the cards drop their output lines first, then shrink to one row per worker.

**The rail (solo)**
- **A rail runs down the left, on screens 110 columns and wider.** It shows:
  - Ryter's name, the project and the branch.
  - The session's title, when it started, and how many turns it has had. A new session gets its title from your first message.
  - **The hat as a large colour block:** `BUILD` green, `PLAN` cyan, `REVIEW` yellow, with what it does and `tab` to switch.
  - The model, its reasoning level, and the context gauge with tokens used out of the window.
  - **Spend:** this turn, the session, the project, the budget, and a bar for each turn.
  - The files the last turn changed, with how many lines were added and removed.
- **The prompt sits in a box of the hat's colour,** with the keys that matter now on its lower edge.
- **`^b` hides the rail** and brings back the top strip and bottom bar. `[ui] panel = false` starts with it hidden. Narrower screens keep the old layout.

## Tried before release

In the TUI at 160×48, with OpenRouter and the crew set to grok-4.7 as lead, glm-5.3-flash for the builders, and qwen3.8-max-prime as auditor, in a small Python project:
- **Solo:** "add a subtract function with a test". The rail showed the turn's cost ($0.019) and listed `calc.py +4 −0` and `test_calc.py +5 −1` under CHANGED.
- **Crew, one task after another:** three tasks, each waiting on the one before. The lanes showed WAITING between steps and the auditor's review arriving under WRITING. All three landed ($0.19).
- **Crew, in parallel:** three independent modules. All three builder cards moved at once, showing files being written with their contents, auditors' reasoning under THINKING, and `$ python3 -m pytest` under RUNNING. PULSE peaked around 270 tokens per second. All three landed, and the project's 20 tests pass ($0.36).
