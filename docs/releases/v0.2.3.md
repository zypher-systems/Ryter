## The fix

Ryter sent no reasoning setting to OpenRouter, and for some models that means unlimited thinking. I measured it with glm-5.3-flashx on a real build plan:

| Reasoning setting | Time to first file write | Reasoning first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| none (0.2.2) | 191 seconds | ~27,000 tokens |
| `medium` (0.2.3 default for build) | 7 seconds | none |
| `high` (0.2.3 default for plan) | 15 seconds | ~640 tokens |

Ryter now always sends one: `high` for planning (the plan hat and the crew's architect), and `medium` for everything that acts (build, review, the lead, builders, auditors). I checked that DeepSeek, Grok, Claude, GLM, GPT, and Qwen models on OpenRouter all accept it. It's sent only to OpenRouter connections.

**You choose it per model.** Press **Tab** on a model in `/models`, on a role in `/crew`, or on a seat in the crew builder to step through `auto → low → medium → high → model's own`. The model card shows the level in use, e.g. `reasoning  auto · medium`. The choice follows the model into every role it plays, and is saved to `~/.ryter/reasoning.toml`.

**Auto** is the default. It picks by role as above, and `[reasoning_effort]` in `config.toml` overrides it per role (`build`, `plan`, `review`, `lead`, `architect`, `builder`, `auditor`). **Model's own** sends nothing.

## Also

- OpenRouter's routing entries (`openrouter/auto` and others, which OpenRouter lists at a price of -1) showed as "$-1000000" and sorted as the cheapest models. `/models` now shows "varies" and sorts them last, and the crew builder's seat lists leave them out.

- **The model card:** `auto · medium` in build, `auto · high` after Tab to plan.
- **Tab** changes a model's level in `/models`, `/crew`, and the crew builder, and saves it.
- **A recipe-box app,** in a fresh folder:
  1. The plan took 25 seconds.
  2. The "switch hat?" prompt appeared, and the answer was `y`.
  3. The build made 13 tool calls with 26 characters of reasoning, finishing in about 50 seconds for $0.01.
  4. The app's own tests pass (5 of 5).
