The screen loses its views: no Hats or Sessions pages, no tabs on the top bar, and no keys to focus the rail. The conversation, the rail and the `/` panels are the whole of it.

## Upgrade

```sh
ryter update
```

Coming from 0.33.0:

- **No views.** The Hats page (`Alt+2`), the Sessions page (`Alt+3`), the three tabs on the top bar and `Alt+1`–`Alt+3` are gone. The top bar carries the brand alone. A hat's description and reasoning level are read in `/models`, where they are set; the saved sessions are `/sessions`, which filters as you type, renames, deletes and starts a new one.
- **The rail is a readout.** `F6` and `F7`, the cursor on the hats and `Enter` to wear one are gone. A click on a hat row still puts that hat on, in place, and `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` reach every hat from the prompt in two presses. The hairline beside the rail is always the rule colour.
- **Nothing said twice.** The hint bar's right end is empty while the rail is up, since the hat and the spend are on the rail; with the rail folded by `F2` it reads `hat · spend`. The welcome names `/sessions` only when the project has an earlier session with something in it to go back to; the count behind it is read again when the open session changes (`/new`, a resume) and after a delete.
- `[ui] caps` rounds the ends of the hat chip; there is no tab to round any more.

## Why

The user asked what the three views gave the application. The Hats page was read only (its `Enter` did nothing) and said what the rail has said since 0.33.0. The Sessions page only resumed, where `/sessions` does everything. `F6`/`F7` was a slower third route to a hat switch. Each one was a place to go with nothing to do there, and the rule since the one-rail release is that nothing on the screen is said twice.

## Good to know

- Below 100 columns there is no rail, so the four hats at once are only in `/models` on a narrow terminal.
- The `/sessions` panel did not take the page's columns (hat, messages, spend, updated).
- The README's screenshots still show the previous screen with its tabs; new ones are owed.
- The light theme's slab is still untried by a person, and a card does not dissolve as it closes.

## Tried before release

The full local check and CI. The real binary driven offline at 132 columns: each of `F6`, `F7`, `Alt+1`, `Alt+2` and `Alt+3` left the idle screen byte for byte as it was; `Tab` changed the hat; `F2` folded and restored the rail; `/sessions` opened with its own keys; a turn ran to its reply. Then, for the welcome's hint: a fresh project showed none, `/new` after a turn showed `/sessions resume one`, deleting that session from `/sessions` took it away again, and the binary started a second time on the same project, with that session on disk, opened with the hint.
