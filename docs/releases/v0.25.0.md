The screen reads as a tool: the watermark is off unless asked for, the hat that is on is the one colored thing, and the transcript's rows are quieter. A specialist's prompt opens on its request, and an audit asked for in the hat shows its cost first.

## Upgrade

```sh
ryter update
```

From 0.8.2 or earlier, run the install script once:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/ryter/main/install.sh | sh
```

Coming from 0.24.1, this looks and behaves differently:

- **The fedora behind the conversation is gone unless you ask for it.** `[ui] watermark = true` in `~/.ryter/config.toml`, or *watermark* in `/settings`, brings it back.
- **Choosing the audit or scribe hat puts its request in the prompt.** "Audit this project", "Document this project". `Enter` sends it; type over it for something else.
- **An audit asked for in the audit hat shows a card with its cost before anything is spent.** Before, only `/audit` did; a message in the hat ran straight away.

## What changed

### The screen

- **One colored thing.** The hat that is on colors its chip, the prompt's rule, its block in the rack, the model's name and the context gauge, as before. The other hats' marks in the top bar and the rack are plain now, a folded turn's dot is dim, and the model's name in the conversation is neutral.
- **Transcript rows are led by space, not dots.** A folded turn ends in what it came to (`✓ 2 tools · 2 files (1 new, 1 changed, +8 −1)  ▸`) without its time and cost, which stay on the turn's closing line that `^o` opens. A tool row shows its time once it is a second or more; `0.0s` beside every edit is gone.
- **The prompt asks its question and no more.** `what should change?`, `what should we plan?`. The keys are on the hint bar beneath it, as before.
- **The rack is headed `HATS`.** A hat with no turns says `no turns yet`, and the build block's check rows leave out a count of zero.
- **The pulse is shown while a turn runs.** It is the last card in the instruments, so nothing above it moves when it comes and goes. Between turns it is not there.

### The specialists

- **A specialist's prompt opens on its request.** `Shift+Tab` to the audit hat and the prompt reads "Audit this project"; `Tab` to the scribe and it reads "Document this project". `Enter` sends it, so the hat and `Enter` are the whole request. The line goes when you leave the hat, and what you typed yourself is never replaced.
- **An audit asked for in the hat asks first.** That line, or anything of your own that begins with "audit", shows the card `/audit` shows: the model, what it will read, a cost range, and after two audits what your last ones with that model cost. `⏎` runs it; `n` spends nothing and says "audit not run". Anything else said in the hat, a question about a finding, is answered without a card, after one line that says what an audit with the hat's model should cost.
- **The range is measured.** It runs from a short audit (six requests, the cache counted) to one that starts the product and uses it key by key (thirty), each request priced on its own. Audits of real projects have taken six to twenty-eight.

## Why

A few people who saw Ryter said the same thing: it works well and looks like a toy. The execution was fine; the register was wrong. A mascot in the work area, four hat colors on every row, dot leaders, help said twice and gauges that read `idle` made the screen playful where a tool people trust at work is quiet. The hats stay; the screen stops performing them.

The specialists came from the terminal-app run of 2026-10-09: a user with an audit specialist on the rack does not type "audit", they switch to the hat and tell it to go, and in that run two audits cost $2.05 after "about $0.10" and "about $0.17" had been said. `DECISIONS.md` 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10 have the choices and what was rejected.

## Good to know

- Nothing about what the hats do changed. `Tab` and `⇧Tab` are where they were, and the hint bar still names them.
- `watermark = true` in `[ui]` is the one setting whose default changed. A file that set it stays as it was.
- The README's screenshots are from before the screen pass.

## Tried before release

The screen: the test harness renders the solo screen at six sizes in each hat, from the code before and after, and the two were read side by side with their colors. The golden snapshots' diff is the changes above and nothing else. The specialists: the real binary against the simulated provider, each hat chosen in turn, the audit's line sent and declined, then sent and run.

## Fixed after review

The release PR's review found one bug and two things worth doing:
- **A specialist's line was put over a queued follow-up.** With a message queued during a turn, choosing the audit or scribe hat filled the composer under the `queued` badge, and Enter sent the line in place of the follow-up. Nothing is put in the composer while a message is queued.
- **"Audit this project" typed by hand was treated as Ryter's line** and replaced on a hat switch. Ryter now remembers the line it put there, and only that line, untouched, goes with the hat.
- **The pulse never showed on a short screen.** It was the last card and the first a short column gave up. Its rows are kept, and the changes card goes first.

Each fix has a test that fails on the reviewed code.
