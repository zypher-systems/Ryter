The screen is rebuilt around one sidebar: every row on it is a measurement, an event or a name. No bar across the top, no fedora anywhere, `RYTER` at the top of the sidebar, plan blue, build green and one colour for every specialist. The transcript's rows are quieter. A specialist's prompt opens on its request, `Shift+Tab` opens the specialists to choose from, and an audit asked for in the hat shows its cost first.

## Upgrade

```sh
ryter update
```

From 0.8.2 or earlier, run the install script once:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/ryter/main/install.sh | sh
```

Coming from 0.24.1, this looks and behaves differently:

- **One column beside the conversation, always.** The hat rack on the left and the instruments on the right are one sidebar on the right, from 100 columns up. It starts with `RYTER`, the session's title and where this is; then the hats as a ledger, what the model is doing now, the context gauge, spend with its cap, the files touched, and what the hat may do.
- **No bar across the top.** The conversation starts on the first row. The name, the title and the folder moved to the sidebar's head; the hats' marks to its ledger.
- **The fedora is gone**, the setting with it. A `config.toml` that still has `watermark` loads without a word.
- **Plan is blue, build is green, every specialist is amber.** A new specialist needs no new colour. Names are lowercase; only `RYTER` is in capitals.
- **Choosing the audit or scribe hat puts its request in the prompt.** "Audit this project", "Document this project". `Enter` sends it; type over it for something else.
- **`Shift+Tab` from plan or build opens the specialists to choose from**, with what each does, what it may do and its model. From a specialist, `Shift+Tab` goes back to the primary hat you last wore.
- **An audit asked for in the audit hat shows a card with its cost before anything is spent.** Before, only `/audit` did; a message in the hat ran straight away.

## What changed

### The sidebar

- **hats.** Every hat, in its colour: plan and build, a hairline marked `specialists`, the specialists under it. The hat on is marked `▸`, and the mark spins while its model works. A hat with turns carries how many and what they cost; a specialist carries its last verdict, `✓` or `✗`; a hat with no turns is its name alone, and nothing says `no turns yet`. A row of dots is the session's shape, one a turn in its hat's colour. `plan.md` and `audit.md` are named only when they exist.
- **now.** The model, then what it is doing in the status row's own words (`thinking`, `writing`, `running cargo test`, `waiting for the model`), then how fast it is writing and how much it has written. While a question waits on you the block is amber and its last row names the question: `allow?  run git push`.
- **context, spend, changes, permissions.** The gauge; the session's cost against its cap and the project's cost; the files touched, a line each, then how many; what this hat may do and the sandbox. The `PULSE` and `GUARD` cards are gone: the pulse is the `now` block, and a guard that read `off · none · none` on every fresh session said nothing.
- **A short screen** gives up rows in order (the files list, the `turns` row, the rate, the permissions, the project's cost) before folding the sidebar away. Under 100 columns the last row is a status line: the hat, its model, the gauge, the cost and its cap. `^b` hides the sidebar, or opens it as a panel where it is folded.

### The rest of the screen

- **The opening screen.** A new session opens on a block at the head of the conversation: `RYTER`, the folder, the branch and what is uncommitted; each hat with what it does and the key that reaches it, `◆ on` beside the one the session opens in; how many sessions this project has to `/sessions` back to. It is drawn, not sent, and gone once there is a turn. The welcome line is retired.
- **Transcript rows are led by space, not dots.** A folded turn ends in what it came to (`✓ 2 tools · 2 files (1 new, 1 changed, +8 −1)  ▸`) without its time and cost, which stay on the turn's closing line that `^o` opens. A tool row shows its time once it is a second or more; `0.0s` beside every edit is gone. The model's name in the conversation is neutral and a folded turn's dot is dim: on the conversation, the hat that is on is the one coloured thing.
- **The prompt asks its question and no more.** `what should change?`, `what should we plan?`. The keys are on the hint bar beneath it, as before.
- **The palette.** The dark theme is muted: one background, hairlines a step apart, plan `#7FA7DB`, build `#8DC29B`, the specialists `#D0B077`, the user's mark a neutral grey-blue so it is not read as the plan hat. Every colour that names a thing meets WCAG AA on the background, and the chip's text on each hat's colour does too. The light theme keeps its structure; nobody has looked at it on this screen yet.

### The specialists

- **A specialist's prompt opens on its request.** Choose the audit hat and the prompt reads "Audit this project"; the scribe, "Document this project". `Enter` sends it, so the hat and `Enter` are the whole request. A session that opens in the audit hat (`[ui] start_hat = "audit"`, `--hat audit`, or one resumed there) opens on the line too. The line goes when you leave the hat, and what you typed yourself is never replaced.
- **The picker.** `Shift+Tab` from plan or build lists the specialists, a row each with what it does, what it may do and its model, on the one you last wore. `↑↓` or typing picks one, `Enter` puts it on, `Esc` leaves things as they were. The sidebar marks the row under the cursor.
- **An audit asked for in the hat asks first.** That line, or anything of your own that begins with "audit", shows the card `/audit` shows: the model, what it will read, a cost range, and after two audits what your last ones with that model cost. `⏎` runs it; `n` spends nothing and says "audit not run". Anything else said in the hat, a question about a finding, is answered without a card, after one line that says what an audit with the hat's model should cost.
- **The range is measured.** It runs from a short audit (six requests, the cache counted) to one that starts the product and uses it key by key (thirty), each request priced on its own. Audits of real projects have taken six to twenty-eight.

## Why

The first group of developers to use Ryter said it works well and looks like a toy. The execution was fine; the register was wrong. A mascot in the work area, four hat colours on every row, dot leaders, help said twice and gauges that read `idle` or `off · none · none` made the screen playful where a tool people trust at work is quiet.

This release is two passes on that. The first stopped the screen performing the hats on the layout as it was. The second is the layout: the user's brief was unique and useful, not what every other harness looks like, with the useful parts visible without opening anything, and the screen was chosen over five rounds of mockups. A gauge that measures is a cockpit; a gauge that reads `off` is a prop. The contract is `docs/sidebar-design.md`; `DECISIONS.md` 2026-10-10 has both passes, the five routes that were drawn and why this one.

The specialists came from the terminal-app run of 2026-10-09: a user with an audit specialist does not type "audit", they switch to the hat and tell it to go, and in that run two audits cost $2.05 after "about $0.10" and "about $0.17" had been said. `DECISIONS.md` 2026-10-09 has the choices and what was rejected.

## Good to know

- Nothing about what the hats do changed. `Tab` is where it was.
- `[ui] panel`, `start_hat`, `theme` and `colors` are unchanged. `[ui] watermark` is retired and ignored.

## Fixed after review

The release PR's first review, of the first pass, found one bug and two things worth doing:
- **A specialist's line was put over a queued follow-up.** With a message queued during a turn, choosing the audit or scribe hat filled the composer under the `queued` badge, and Enter sent the line in place of the follow-up. Nothing is put in the composer while a message is queued.
- **"Audit this project" typed by hand was treated as Ryter's line** and replaced on a hat switch. Ryter now remembers the line it put there, and only that line, untouched, goes with the hat.
- **The pulse never showed on a short screen.** It was the last card and the first a short column gave up. The pulse card has since gone with the sidebar screen; its words are the `now` block's.

The second review found two gaps in the specialist opening:
- **A session resumed from inside Ryter did not open on the line.** `/resume` and the sessions list set the hat and left the prompt empty; only the command line's resume opened on it. Now every resume does.
- **A panel that emptied the prompt kept Ryter's claim on the line.** Open the sessions list, which clears the prompt, then type "Audit this project" yourself, and the next hat switch replaced your words as if Ryter had put them there. Ryter's line is its own only while it stands untouched: a key, a paste or a panel that changes the prompt lets it go, and the words typed afterwards are yours.

Each fix has a test that fails on the reviewed code.
