The three views of 0.6.0 are now in sight. A strip across the top names them and shows which one you're in, and crew mode shows its board from the start.

## Upgrade

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/ryter/main/install.sh | sh
```

## What was wrong

0.6.0 had three views, but you could only find one:
- **The workbench was behind `^T`,** and nothing on screen said so.
- **The crew board only appeared once the crew had a plan.** A crew session that started with a question looked just like solo mode.

## What changed

- **A view strip across the top:** `chat`, `changes ^t`, and `crew board`, with the view on screen lit in the mode's color. From solo mode, the crew board shows `/crew`, the way to get there.
- **The crew board shows as soon as you're in crew mode.** Before there's a plan, it says "no plan yet" and what will appear there once there is one.
- **In crew mode, the workbench replaces the board** rather than squeezing in under it. Esc brings the board back.
- **`^t changes` is in the bottom bar's keys** too, when there's room.

## Tried before release

In the TUI:
- **Solo mode:** the strip showed `chat · changes ^t · crew board /crew`, with `chat` lit.
- **`/crew`:** the board appeared at once with "no plan yet", and the strip lit `crew board`.
- **`^T` in crew mode:** it opened the full workbench, and the strip lit `changes`. Esc went back to the board.
