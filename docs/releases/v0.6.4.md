The model picker opens at once, even when a provider is slow to send its model list, and a slow list no longer holds up your messages.

## Upgrade

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/ryter/main/install.sh | sh
```

## What was wrong

OpenRouter's model list endpoint was slow: it took 75 seconds or more to send its catalog, most of a megabyte, where it usually takes under one. Ryter handled that badly:
- **The picker spun until the whole list arrived,** every time you opened it. Ryter kept no copy of the last list.
- **A slow download was never cut off,** because the limit only fires when nothing arrives at all, and a trickle kept it going.
- **While the list downloaded, a message you sent waited behind it.**

## What changed

- **Ryter asks OpenRouter only for the models that can use tools.** That's what the build hat and the crew can drive. That list came back complete in 0.16 seconds while the full catalog stalled. The picker now fills in under a second, with the same models as before, less the ones it used to dim for not taking tools. You can still set one of those by id in `config.toml`.
- **Ryter keeps the last list each connection sent,** and the picker opens on it at once. The corner says how old it is (`from 2 h ago · refreshing`) while the fresh list downloads, and the fresh list replaces it when it arrives.
- **The download runs on its own,** so your messages go through while it runs.
- **After 30 seconds the picker says the provider is slow** (`openrouter slow · still downloading`), and the download keeps going for up to 5 minutes so the next open is instant. If it never comes, the picker keeps the last list and says so.
- **The crew builder's model lists work the same way.**

## Tried before release

In the TUI, on OpenRouter, while its full catalog was stalling (plain `curl` took over 90 seconds without finishing):
- **`/models` with nothing saved:** the list filled in 0.65 seconds with 295 models, and was saved.
- **`/models` again:** the list showed at once, marked `from a moment ago · refreshing`, while the fresh one came in behind it.
- **A message sent while an earlier version was still downloading the list** was answered in 4 seconds; before, it waited behind the download.
- **`ryter models`:** it answered in 0.26 seconds; before, it didn't finish within 60.
