Two fixes to what the TUI shows: the cursor while you type, and how a stopped turn ends.

## Upgrade

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/ryter/main/install.sh | sh
```

## The cursor moves when you type a space

The cursor stayed after the last letter you typed until the next word began. After typing `fix the` and a space, it sat right after "the", so it was hard to tell where the next letter would go.

The message box wraps long lines by word and leaves the spaces at the end of each line out, and the cursor position was measured only up to the last letter. Now it counts the spaces too:

- at the end of a message;
- in an empty box;
- on a line that is only spaces;
- at the point where a long line wraps.

## A stopped turn says "stopped"

After Esc, the chat said "cancelled" but the status line said `✓ done`. It now says `⊘ stopped`, and a turn that failed keeps its failed status too.

## Tried before release

In the TUI:

- **Typing:** after `fix the` and three spaces, v0.2.6 showed `› fix the█` and v0.2.7 shows `› fix the   █`.
- **Esc during a long command:** the status line ended with `⊘ stopped · 1 tool · 0:07`.
