A new screen. The hat you are wearing now colors the whole screen, each hat keeps its own tally in a hat rack on the left, and a new session opens in the plan hat.

## Upgrade

```sh
ryter update
```

From 0.8.2 or earlier, run the install script once:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zypher-systems/ryter/main/install.sh | sh
```

Coming from 0.12.0, these behave differently:

- **A new session opens in the plan hat,** not build. To go back, open `/settings` and set *start in* to `build`, or put `start_hat = "build"` under `[ui]` in `~/.ryter/config.toml`. `ryter --hat build` does it for one run.
- **Headless runs are unchanged.** `ryter -p` is in the build hat unless `--hat` says otherwise, so scripts keep working.
- **The rail is gone.** What it showed is now in two side columns and a bar across the top. `^b` still hides and shows them.
- **The dark theme's colors changed.** A theme file of your own is untouched.

## What was wrong

- **The screen said little at a glance.** The hat was a word in a side rail. Which hat had done what, and what each had cost, was not on screen anywhere: spend was one figure for the session.
- **A session started by changing things.** Ryter opened in the build hat, so the first message went to a model that may edit files. Reading and proposing first took a `Shift+Tab`.
- **The rail was one column for everything,** and at 110 columns and under it went away whole, leaving one bar at the foot.

## What changed

- **The hat is the color.** Plan is cyan, build green, review amber, test violet. The hat that is on colors the bar's chip, the prompt, the model's name, the context gauge and its own block in the rack. Everything else stays the same under every hat, so the screen doesn't jump when you press `Tab`.
- **A fedora behind the conversation,** in the hat's color, as a faint tint under the text. It never changes a letter or a letter's color, and an edit's green and red rows stay as they are. `[ui] watermark = false`, or *watermark* in `/settings`, turns it off. It is left out on 16-color and no-color terminals.
- **A bar across the top** names the four hats in the order `Tab` goes round them: `◆` for the one that is on, `●` for one that has had a turn this session, `○` for one that hasn't. Then the session's title, the folder and the branch.
- **The hat rack,** down the left on screens 132 columns and wider. One block a hat, always the same four:
  - the hat's model, how many turns it has had, and what it has cost;
  - plan: plans approved and rejected;
  - build: files and lines changed this session, and the latest test run;
  - review: verdicts passed and failed;
  - test: checks passed and failed.

  It is the same height at forty turns as at four. A resumed session shows the rack it had.
- **The instruments,** down the right on screens 100 columns and wider: the model your next message goes to and its connection, the context gauge, what the session and the project have cost, the budget, the sandbox and what this hat may do, and the files that are uncommitted.
- **Narrow screens.** Under 132 columns the hat rack folds away and the bar counts each hat's turns. Under 100 the instruments fold away too and the last row becomes a status line. On those screens `^b` opens both as a panel.
- **Where a session starts.** `[ui] start_hat`, or *start in* at the top of `/settings`: `plan` (the default), `build`, `review`, or `last`, the hat this project's most recent session ended in. A resumed session opens in the hat it was left in.
- **`ryter --hat` works for the TUI,** not only with `-p`.
- **An offer to switch hats is in that hat's color:** the review offer amber, the test offer violet.
- **What each hat said is named in its color** in the conversation, and a folded turn's mark is in the color of the hat it ran in.

## Tried before release

- **The whole test suite** on Linux: 642 tests pass. The new ones cover each hat's figures, the rack's fixed height, the columns folding at each width, the hat's color on every accented part, the watermark leaving text and edit rows untouched, `^b` at three widths, a resumed session's rack, and the start-hat setting.
- **A real session in a terminal,** on real models through OpenRouter: Ryter opened in the plan hat, a plan was written by `claude-opus-5.5` and approved, `glm-5.3` built it and ran its tests, and the session was closed and resumed. The rack, each hat's spend and the uncommitted files were right at each step, and after the resume. It cost $0.12.
- **At 160, 110 and 80 columns,** with the hat rack panel opened by `^b` at the two narrower sizes.
- **In truecolor, 16 colors and no color.** The colors on screen were read back from the terminal and matched the theme. With 16 colors and with none, there is no watermark, and the hat that is on is still marked.
- **Two faults were found that way and fixed:** the plan's approval panel overlapped the right-hand column, and the watermark's edge drew marks in the gaps between words.

- **Not yet tried:**
  - A review or a test in a real session on the new screen. Tests cover both hats' figures; the live session stopped after the build.
  - The light theme by a person. Its contrast over the watermark is checked by a test.
  - Anything on a Mac by hand.
  - A 256-color terminal by hand.
