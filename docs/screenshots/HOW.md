# The screenshots

`build.png` and `plan.png` are the README's pictures of the screen. They are staged, not taken from a paid session: the real binary runs in tmux at 152×40 against `scripts/simulated_provider.py`, with one connection pointed at it and a model per hat named in its config, a small Express project with a plan on record, and the build turn's edits and reply scripted. The screen is captured with `tmux capture-pane -e`, each cell drawn at a fixed width in JetBrains Mono at 16 px, and rendered by headless Chromium to 1467×860.

The model names on the rail are the seats the config names; the replies are the script's, not those models'. The costs are what Ryter charged the scripted token counts at the prices in that config. Retake them when the screen changes.
